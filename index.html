Tower of Hanoi

Move the whole stack to another peg. Move one disc at a time, and never place a bigger disc on a smaller one.

How to play

  • Click or tap a peg to pick up its top disc, then click the peg where it should go.
  • On a keyboard, press 1, 2 or 3 to choose a peg. Z undoes a move.
  • The fewest possible moves for n discs is 2n − 1.
